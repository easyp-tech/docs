The separate [package-manager comparison](../cli/package-manager/easyp-vs-buf) covers that different scope; its claims should not be substituted for this date-specific service comparison. ## Where BSR is stronger

The largest difference is not execution speed: it is the product around execution. Generated SDKs let consumers install packages rather than running a generator and maintaining output commits; the supported package surfaces include Python indexes rather than publication to the public PyPI service, and Swift includes a per-version Git fallback for Xcode. These are services absent from EasyP API Service, not equivalent to its Go client SDK. [SDK overview](https://buf.build/docs/bsr/generated-sdks/) · [Python](https://buf.build/docs/bsr/generated-sdks/python/) · [Swift](https://buf.build/docs/bsr/generated-sdks/swift/).

BSR also separates organizational permissions and supports governed schema publication. Enterprise policy enforcement and commit review are more than running a linter on a laptop. However, a pending review does not mean that every explicit commit-hash reference becomes inaccessible; use the documented review/default-label semantics rather than claiming a universal data-access block. [Policies](https://buf.build/docs/bsr/checks/policies/) · [Commit review](https://buf.build/docs/bsr/checks/review-commits/).

BSR’s sandbox model is a real advantage over executing registered commands with the service account’s privileges. EasyP’s output/time limits and path checks do not provide equivalent isolation. Conversely, a trusted generator that genuinely needs external state may be unsuitable for BSR remote execution. That is a workload trade-off, not a reason to label lack of isolation a security feature. [Custom-plugin constraints](https://buf.build/docs/bsr/remote-plugins/custom-plugins/) · [On-prem sandbox configuration](https://buf.build/docs/bsr/admin/on-prem/configuration/).

## Cost on the access date

On **2026-09-23**, Buf lists Community as free with one private repository of up to 100 types; Teams is **USD 0.50/type/month**; Pro is **USD 5/type/month with a USD 3,000/month minimum**; Enterprise requires a quote. “Types” include messages, enums, and RPCs; billing scope differs by plan. These are the published BSR prices, not a remote-generator-only price or a forecast. [Live pricing and billing definitions](https://buf.build/pricing).

EasyP Community does not require an Enterprise token, but it does require infrastructure and operation. This audit did not establish a public Enterprise price or a support SLA for EasyP. Do not infer either from the source license. Compare database/storage backups, patching, plugin validation, incident response, and staff time alongside subscriptions. A license that permits your intended hosted offering is a separate question from a runnable binary; see [Licensing](./authentication-and-licensing).

## Availability, privacy, and claims we do not make

A public BSR call depends on the selected external service and network path. Buf publishes a [status page](https://www.bufstatus.com/), linked by its [support documentation](https://buf.build/docs/bsr/admin/instance/support/). This comparison does not infer uptime percentages from an incomplete incident archive or present an outage ranking. A self-hosted deployment moves dependency management to your operators; it does not eliminate outages.

Neither local hosting nor an air-gapped design certifies compliance with a country’s data requirements, including Russia’s. Evaluate actual data flows, legal obligations, procurement/export availability, telemetry, and the chosen contract separately. We did not validate regional sales eligibility, production contracts, universal sandbox time/memory ceilings, or source-availability terms of the BSR backend. No unsupported “BSR has no X” claims are used as EasyP advantages.

## When to choose BSR

Choose BSR when you need generated SDK distribution, shared schema discovery/documentation, organizational authorization, or enforced registry policy, and can select the plan and deployment that provide them. Prefer managed operation when maintaining a plugin executor and its dependencies would distract from the work you are buying the service to solve. [BSR overview](https://buf.build/docs/bsr/) · [SDKs](https://buf.build/docs/bsr/generated-sdks/) · [Pricing](https://buf.build/pricing).

## When to choose EasyP

Choose EasyP when the required product is a trusted generation endpoint under your operational control rather than a full schema platform, and the Community ceilings or a valid Enterprise license fit your load. Confirm your real clients can meet the chosen authentication policy, validate the selected plugin recipes, and budget for the absence of a sandbox and cross-replica cache coordination. Local generation remains the simpler third option when neither shared service is necessary.

[Migration and reverse migration](./migrating-from-bsr) provides a reversible path; [Security](./security) describes the execution boundary you are accepting.
